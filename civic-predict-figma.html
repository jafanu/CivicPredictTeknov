The Problem

Reactive Systems Leave Communities Waiting

Current public-service mechanisms wait for problems to occur and be reported before taking action.

Delayed Response

Issues are only addressed after citizens discover and report them, causing unnecessary delays.

Repeated Problems

Without pattern analysis, the same issues recur in the same locations repeatedly.

Unoptimized Resources

Government resources are spread thin instead of being directed to highest-priority areas.

Risk Intelligence

Public Service Risk Score

Each issue is analyzed and assigned a score from 0 to 100 based on multiple risk factors.

50-74

25-49
Medium
