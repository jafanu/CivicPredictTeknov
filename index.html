The Problem

Reactive Systems Leave Communities Waiting

Current public-service mechanisms wait for problems to occur and be reported before taking action.

Delayed Response

Issues are only addressed after citizens discover and report them, causing unnecessary delays in resolution.

Repeated Problems

Without pattern analysis, the same issues recur in the same locations, wasting public resources.

Unoptimized Resources

Government resources are spread thin instead of being directed to the highest-priority areas.

Key Features

Intelligent Decision Support for Government

Public Service Risk Score

Every issue receives a 0–100 risk score based on complaint frequency, historical patterns, severity, and potential public impact.

Interactive Risk Map

Geographic visualization of risks across the region with color-coded markers and detailed popups.

AI Predictions

Forecast which problems will worsen, which are recurring, and identify emerging risks before they escalate.

Smart Recommendations

Actionable, department-specific recommendations generated by AI based on risk analysis and historical data.

Advanced Analytics

Comprehensive dashboards with trend analysis, department performance, and geographic distribution insights.

AI Decision Assistant

Chat-based assistant that helps officials query data, understand risks, and make informed decisions.

Risk Intelligence

Public Service Risk Score

Each issue is analyzed and assigned a score from 0 to 100 based on multiple risk factors.

75–100
Critical

Immediate attention required. High probability of escalating impact.

50–74
High

Urgent action needed. Risk of significant public impact.

25–49
Medium

Monitor closely. May require intervention if conditions change.

0–24
Low

Stable condition. Include in routine maintenance schedule.
