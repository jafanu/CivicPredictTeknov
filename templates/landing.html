Key Features

Intelligent Decision Support for Government

Public Service Risk Score

Every issue receives a 0-100 risk score based on complaint frequency, historical patterns, severity, and potential public impact.

Interactive Risk Map

Geographic visualization of risks across the region with color-coded markers and detailed popups.

AI Predictions

Forecast which problems will worsen, which are recurring, and identify emerging risks before they escalate.

Smart Recommendations

Actionable, department-specific recommendations generated by AI based on risk analysis and historical data.
